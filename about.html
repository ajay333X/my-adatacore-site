About Adatacore

Human data operations should be inspectable.

Adatacore is built around a simple idea: quality comes from the operating system around human work—qualification, instructions, controlled access, review, feedback and delivery—not from volume alone.

What we are building

A platform that connects contributor onboarding, project access, production, layered QA, client review, Trust & Safety and operational analytics.

Principles

Designed for accountable human-in-the-loop work.

These principles guide current product decisions and public claims.

01

Quality before scale

Workflows should define qualification and review before optimizing throughput.

02

Truthful capability claims

We prefer narrower, verifiable platform capabilities over inflated marketing promises.

03

Human decisions with context

Review outcomes, changes and security signals should remain explainable and auditable.

04

Separate responsibilities

Contributor, reviewer, client and administrative access should be scoped to the work each role needs.

05

Transparent limitations

Security signals such as IP location are approximate and should not be presented as stronger evidence than they are.

06

Operational improvement

Metrics should help teams find backlog, quality and workflow issues instead of merely producing decorative dashboards.

Need a human data workflow?

For client work, start with the problem, data type, language and quality bar. For contributor work, use the qualification flow.
