Cookie & Storage Notice

Effective: 3 September 2026

This notice explains how Adatacore uses cookies and browser storage on the public website and authenticated platform.

Public website

The public Adatacore website does not currently use advertising or behavioral-targeting cookies. For basic funnel measurement, the public site creates a random identifier in sessionStorage. It lasts only for the browser tab/session and is sent to Adatacore as a one-way hash together with an allow-listed event name, page path and referrer host.

Authenticated platform

Sign-in and protected product experiences may use cookies or browser storage required by authentication, session management, security, interface preferences and normal product operation. Removing required session storage can sign you out or prevent protected features from working.

Local preferences

Some interface preferences, such as theme selection or temporary workflow state, may be stored locally in your browser so the product can remember your choice.

Third-party services

Infrastructure providers may use their own essential cookies or storage when necessary to provide hosting, authentication, security or related platform functions. See the Privacy Policy for more information about data handling.

Your controls

You can clear site data through your browser settings. Doing so can remove saved preferences and active sign-in sessions. Because Adatacore does not currently use advertising cookies on the public site, there is no advertising-cookie preference center at this time.

Changes

If Adatacore introduces non-essential cookies in the future, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated before those cookies are relied upon.
