What we evaluate

A strong take sounds natural before it sounds impressive.

We are not looking for a radio-announcer voice. We want speech that feels believable, controlled and appropriate to the situation in the script.

01

Natural delivery

Avoid robotic reading, artificial pauses and forced emotion. Speak as if the conversation is actually happening.

02

Clean audio

Use a quiet room and stable microphone distance. Avoid fan noise, echo, clipping, clicks and distortion.

03

Accurate Hindi

Pronounce the written words clearly and naturally. Do not skip, replace or improvise parts of the assigned script.

04

Believable emotion

Follow the direction without overacting. Emotion should support the meaning rather than overpower the words.

Before you start

Set yourself up for one clean, uninterrupted take.

The assigned Hindi script is designed to take roughly two minutes at a natural conversational pace. You can listen to your recording and retry before anything is submitted.

01

Use headphones if your environment needs themPrevent speaker playback or notifications from leaking into your recording.

02

Choose the quietest room availableTurn off fans, music and other avoidable background noise where possible.

03

Use your normal speaking voiceDo not imitate an announcer or exaggerate the emotional direction.

04

Keep about the same microphone distanceThis helps us evaluate your real recording quality consistently.

How it works

A simple, secure application flow.

Your private script stays attached to your account, so refreshing or retrying does not give you a different assessment.

01Account

Sign in or create your account

We use your Adatacore account to protect the assessment, prevent accidental duplicate submissions and keep review status private.

02Assessment

Receive your assigned Hindi script

A script is selected from the assessment pool and stored against your application. Different applicants are distributed across different scripts.

03Recording

Record, listen and retry if needed

Select your microphone, record directly in the browser, listen back and make another take before submitting if necessary.

04Review

Submit for quality review

Your recording enters the application review queue. Selection depends on assessment quality and current project requirements.
