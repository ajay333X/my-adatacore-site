What we listen for

Sound like a person, not a performance demo.

The best submissions feel conversational, controlled and technically clean. You do not need a studio if your recording environment is quiet and your microphone is clear.

01

Natural delivery

Avoid robotic reading, announcement tone and forced acting. Let the emotion fit the situation.

02

Clean audio

Use a quiet room. Avoid fan noise, echo, clipping, mouth clicks and changing microphone distance.

03

Clear Hindi

Pronounce every word accurately and naturally. Don’t rush difficult words or swallow endings.

04

Good pacing

Use realistic pauses and steady energy. Fast does not mean energetic, and slow does not mean emotional.
