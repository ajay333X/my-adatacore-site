Contributor compensation control

Configure project compensation rules, record reviewer recommendations, and authorize compensation exceptions without changing task quality outcomes.

Controlled rollout: setting a policy does not automatically alter existing contributor balances or release payments. Payment disbursement and legacy reconciliation remain disabled until verified.

Project compensation rules

Review-side compensation recommendation

Reviewers can recommend that a submitted task be paid despite rejection. A finance administrator must separately authorize the recommendation.

Recommendation and authorization are logged separately. Authorization alone does not transfer money.
