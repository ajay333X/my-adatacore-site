FAQ

Before you get started.

Common questions from teams and contributors.

Current platform workflows support voice and speech work, transcription and language review, multimodal annotation, human evaluation and quality-review operations. A project can use one capability or combine several stages.

New contributors create an account, complete profile information, choose a language and track, and complete the relevant assessment. Submitted applications are reviewed before mapped project access is granted.

Quality controls depend on the project. Adatacore supports staged review, reviewer notes, changes requested, L1/L2-style workflows, task limits and project-specific guidelines rather than treating every workflow as one undifferentiated queue.

The public Trust Center explains current account security, project access, storage, authentication and Trust & Safety practices. For platform availability, use the public Status page.
