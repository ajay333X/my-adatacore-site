Account & profile

Getting into Adatacore

Open the Adatacore sign-in page and choose the account-creation option. New contributors are routed through profile setup, language and skill selection, and the relevant assessment before their application enters review.
Check the email address carefully, then check Spam or Junk folders. If the message still does not arrive, return to the sign-in flow and use the available resend option before opening an account-specific support request.
Profile information is reused across your contributor profile and application workflow. Required information helps identify the account and determine project eligibility; optional information such as education or occupation adds useful context for reviewers.

Voice Acting

Recording assessments and project audio

The browser must receive microphone permission before Adatacore can show available audio inputs. Preparing the microphone also lets you confirm the selected device before recording.
Yes. The application recorder lets you stop, replay and retry your sample before pressing the final submission button.
Follow the script and delivery instructions, keep pronunciation clear, maintain consistent microphone distance, and aim for natural performance rather than an announcement-style or unnecessarily exaggerated delivery.
Transcription

Audio and transcript work

The assessment provides audio playback, a five-second rewind control, multiple playback speeds, a transcription editor and a final submit action.
Smart Split can accept a larger source locally in the browser and divide it into smaller pause-aware chunks before upload. This keeps generated chunks manageable while avoiding an abrupt cut at the exact target time whenever a usable quiet boundary is nearby.
Keep as one preserves the audio as a single module. Smart Split creates multiple linked modules from one source and is designed for longer recordings that are easier to transcribe and review in smaller sections.
Projects & tasks

Access and assignments

Creating an account does not automatically grant access to every project. You may need an approved application or qualification and an active project-access record before the project appears.
Projects can use task limits and quality-based controls. The limit can depend on project policy, current workload, quality history, reviewer capacity and available inventory.
L1 generally represents production work, while L2 represents a review or audit layer. Exact responsibilities depend on the project instructions and your assigned role.
Payments

Earnings and payment states

Work approval and payment completion are separate states. An approved submission can still move through pending, scheduled, processing and paid stages depending on the project payment workflow.
Use the signed-in Workspace support flow and include the relevant project, task or submission information. Account-specific payment questions should be handled through authenticated support rather than a public form.
